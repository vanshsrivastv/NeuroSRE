NeuroSRE console: design samples

Same example data, six directions. Each has a light / dark switch. Open one, then come back here.

New hybrids

E · FLIGHT DECK

Incident story + live agent pipeline

D's three panes, C's big numbers, B's dense rows and terminal, plus a live LangGraph pipeline, an evidence-gate panel, a service map with flowing traffic and a Replay button.

  • Shows how the AI thinks, which is what the project is about
  • Strongest for a live demo and viva questions
F · TOPOLOGY

Service map + blast radius

A's calm console around a large dependency map with live request flow, the failing service, the impacted upstream and a blast-radius note.

  • Most visually striking at a glance
  • Incident and approval in a side panel

Original samples

A · CONSOLE

Calm operations console

Left navigation, panels, balanced density. Closest to Datadog or Grafana.

  • Best all-rounder; easy to read on a projector
  • Services as a strip of cards with trend lines
B · NOC

Control-room view

Top tabs, a dense service-health table with gauges, terminal-style log. Dark by default.

  • Most "engineering" feel; shows the most data at once
  • Busiest of the four
C · SWISS

Editorial, typographic

Big numbers, hairline grids, black and white plus one red accent. Light by default.

  • Most distinctive and presentation-friendly
  • Less dense; scrolls vertically
D · INCIDENT-FIRST

Three-pane triage

Incident queue, the incident story in the centre, live system and chaos controls on the right.

  • Best for narrating a demo: one incident, start to finish
  • Like PagerDuty or incident.io
